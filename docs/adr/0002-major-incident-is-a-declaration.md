# Major incident Yes is a declaration, never an inference

Declaring a major incident triggers special arrangements and may be true for one service and not another. An extractor that stamps Yes from casualty counts or “this looks big” is a false declaration.

Speech that says “this is a major incident” may fill Yes as Estimated until the officer confirms. Anything else leaves Major incident Unknown or explicitly No. The confirm UI always requires an explicit Yes / No / leave Unknown before SEND.
