# The right pane is the official JESIP completion form

JESIP already defines how a M/ETHANE is filled: seven prose boxes (plus DATE/TIME; Major incident is Yes/No with time of declaration). The JESIP app’s extra casualty-sign screen is not that form.

Voice and the local model only write into those boxes. We add a Transcript on the left, Provenance chips on each box, and confirm/SEND. We do not add fields, split Access or N, or put Quote on the form.
